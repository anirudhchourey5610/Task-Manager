# Design System: Lunar Slate (TaskFlow Premium)
**Project ID:** 9175675145564849037

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
The "Lunar Slate" theme is a sophisticated, high-contrast aesthetic designed for professional productivity. It combines the depth of obsidian space with the precision of technical slate. The atmosphere is **Commanding, Crisp, and Focused**, using subtle glassmorphism and deep shadows to create a sense of digital hierarchy and premium quality.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
* **Deep Obsidian Space (#0B0F19):** Primary background color. Provides a solid, non-distracting foundation.
* **Slate Command Center (#161B22):** Secondary background for sidebars and cards. Distinguishes interactive zones from the main workspace.
* **Cyber Blue Energy (#3B82F6):** Primary action color. Used for buttons, active states, and critical highlights.
* **Lunar Silver (#E2E8F0):** Primary text color. High legibility against dark backgrounds.
* **Ghost Slate (#94A3B8):** Secondary text and labels. Reduces visual noise for non-essential information.
* **Emerald Success (#10B981):** Success indicators and completed tasks.
* **Crimson Alert (#EF4444):** Errors, deletions, and high-priority warnings.

## 3. Typography Rules
* **Primary Font:** Inter (Sans-serif). Known for its mathematical precision and readability.
* **Headers:** Semibold weight with slightly tighter letter-spacing for a modern "tech" feel.
* **Body:** Medium weight for optimal legibility.
* **Character:** Clean, minimalist, and utilitarian.

## 4. Component Stylings
* **Buttons:** Gently rounded corners (8px radius). High-contrast background for primary actions; Ghost Slate border for secondary. Smooth hover transition to a brighter shade.
* **Cards/Containers:** "Slate Command Center" background with a whisper-soft translucent border (1px solid #30363D). Subtle "elevation" shadow for depth.
* **Inputs/Forms:** Deep-set appearance with a 2px Cyber Blue focus ring. Clear, high-contrast text.

## 5. Layout Principles
* **Whitespace Strategy:** Generous "Breathing Room" (32px default margins). Elements are never cramped.
* **Grid Alignment:** Precise 8px grid system for all padding and margins.
* **Interactive Layering:** Sidebars and Modals use subtle backdrop blurs (Glassmorphism) to maintain context while focusing attention.
